Membership Application

Join a community of people with disability advocating for their rights and wellbeing.

Join our community

Membership is free and open to people with disability.

Become a member of the Disability Advocacy Service and join a community of people with disability advocating for their rights and wellbeing.

Membership benefits

  • Access to free advocacy services
  • Participation in community events and awareness sessions
  • Regular updates on disability rights and advocacy
  • Connection with other people with disability
  • Support and information for navigating NDIS and other services

Apply online

Membership application

Complete the form below to apply. If you would rather talk it through, call us on (08) 8953 – 1422.

Prefer to print or email it? Download the fillable PDF

No email? You can still apply.

Our online form is set up to email you a copy, so if you don't have an address we'll need a phone number or postal address instead. Two easy ways to apply:

  • Call us on (08) 8953 – 1422 and we'll take your application over the phone.
  • Download the fillable PDF — it has a "No email" option — and post or drop it in to us at 12 Gregory Terrace, Alice Springs NT 0870.
