Client Referral Form

Refer yourself or someone you know to our free, confidential advocacy services.

If you or someone you know could benefit from our free advocacy services, please complete the appropriate referral form below. All referrals are handled confidentially.

Which referral do you need?

General advocacy referral

Prefer to print or email it? Download the fillable PDF

No email? You can still refer.

Our online form is set up to email a copy of the referral, so if the person doesn't have an email address we'll need a phone number or postal address instead. Two easy ways to refer:

  • Call us on (08) 8953 – 1422 and we'll complete the referral with you over the phone.
  • Download the fillable PDF — it has a "No email" option — and post or drop it in to us at 12 Gregory Terrace, Alice Springs NT 0870.

Prefer to talk?

You can also make a referral by contacting us directly. We will follow up with the person to discuss how we can support them.
